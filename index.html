Privacy policy

MellonMind

A quiet place to empty your head. Say or type a thought, and MellonMind tidies it up and files it, using Apple Intelligence on your device. It also keeps a recipe book that cooks with you, with timers and Cook mode.

Support

Questions, problems or ideas: Trevorjmell3@proton.me. Please say which device and iOS version you use, and what you were doing.

Good to know

  • No account is needed, and the developer receives none of your notes. See the privacy policy.
  • Sorting and cleanup need Apple Intelligence (iPhone 15 Pro or newer, or an iPad or Mac with an M-series chip). Without it your notes are still saved, and another device that has it can sort them.
  • Your notes sync through your own iCloud account.
