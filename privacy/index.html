Unspool Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Unspool is a note-capture app. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and it doesn't track you. The developer does not receive your notes, recordings or any other data from the app.

What the app handles, and where it goes

DataWhyWhere it goes
Notes, text, photos, files, voice recordingsThey are your notesStored on your device. If iCloud sync is on (the default), they are also stored in your private iCloud account, encrypted by Apple's CloudKit. Only you and your signed-in devices can read them.
Microphone and speechRecording and transcribing voice notesTranscription happens on your device.
AI sorting (titles, tags, cleanup, reminders)Organise your notesDone by Apple Intelligence on your device unless you turn on Cloud AI (below). If you turn on Private Cloud, requests may run on Apple's Private Cloud Compute, which Apple designs so that your data isn't stored or accessible to Apple.
Cloud AI with your own key (optional, off by default)Sort notes, tidy recipes, write recipe steps and fix mistakes with a model you choose, for example on a phone without Apple IntelligenceOnly if you turn it on in Settings → Cloud AI and add your own API key for Claude (Anthropic), OpenAI or Gemini (Google). Then, for the things you chose, the text of the note or recipe being worked on (and your tag names with a few short examples of notes filed under them) is sent from your device straight to that company with your key, under their terms and privacy policy, and is billed to your account. The key is kept only in this device's Keychain: it is not synced, not backed up and not seen by the developer. With a key added, a long note that holds several separate thoughts (a ramble) is also sent to be split into separate notes (voice dump); whether it looks like a ramble is decided on your device first, and nothing is sent for ordinary short notes. Voice recordings, photos, your other notes and recipes in the Family book are never sent, not even through a feature such as Fix this recipe. You can turn it off or remove the key at any time.
Calendar (optional)Add plans from your notes to your calendar and keep them in stepStays on your devices (and your own iCloud Calendar, if you use it). Only a title, place and link to the note are written. The app only looks for events it created.
Contacts (optional)Link people you mention in a note to your contacts, and fill in a person's birthday, photo and numberYou can add a person from a contact with Apple's contact picker. If you also allow Contacts access (optional, asked from a note or from Settings → Places & Calendar), the app matches names mentioned in your notes against your address book on your device to link the people you mention; to do that it can search the whole address book, but only on your device. For a contact that is linked, the birthday, a small copy of the photo, and the phone number or email are copied onto the person's page and stored on your device and in your private iCloud (like your notes). Nothing from your contacts goes to the developer or to any AI model. On the Mac there is no picker, so linking a contact there needs that access. You can also add people without any contact.
Location (optional)Find places for your notes; arrive/leave and leave-now remindersUsed on your device. To look up a place, the place name you wrote is sent to Apple Maps with the area around your home or last known location. "Leave now" alerts ask Apple Maps for the travel time between where you are and the place. "Always" access is used only for the arrive/leave reminders you turn on.
Link previewsShow a title and picture for a link in a noteApple's link-preview service fetches the page and its picture from your device when you open a note or the Board; the request goes to that site.
Recipes (#recipe notes)Turn a recipe you saved into ingredients and steps with timersCleaned up on your device by Apple Intelligence. If your note is a link, or contains one next to a #recipe, the app downloads that public web page from your device (only that address is requested, with no cookies and nothing from your notes) and reads the recipe on your device. A note that is only a link is also checked this way, in the background, to see whether it is a recipe; one-time links (sign-in, confirm, unsubscribe) are skipped. Photos of a recipe are read on your device. Timers are set with the system's alarm and notification features on your device. Hands-free Cook mode (off until you turn it on) listens for short spoken commands such as "next" using on-device speech recognition; nothing is recorded or kept. Nutrition estimates are calculated on your device from a nutrition table (USDA FoodData Central, public domain) stored inside the app; your ingredients are never sent anywhere. The shopping list is an ordinary checklist note in your own notes and iCloud.
Camera (optional)Scan a printed recipe pageUsed only when you tap "Scan a page". The photo is read on your device and stays in your note; it is not sent anywhere else.
Siri and ShortcutsVoice commands such as "Next step in Unspool" and starting a noteHandled on your device. The commands read the recipe you have open and can start its timer.
Family recipe book and shared shopping list (optional)Share recipes, and a shopping list, with people you inviteUses Apple's iCloud sharing. Only the recipes and the shopping list you put in the Family book are shared, only with the people you invite, and they can add and edit them. They are stored in your iCloud account (and in the invited people's iCloud for their copies); nothing goes to the developer. Leaving or stopping the share removes the copies.
People pages (optional)Keep gift ideas, things to ask, facts and birthdays for people you chooseOnly people you add get a page; nothing is imported in bulk. Matching a name in a note to a person happens on your device, and the list of people is never sent to an AI model, including the one you may add with your own key. People pages are stored on your device and in your private iCloud, encrypted by Apple's CloudKit. Birthday reminders are scheduled on your device and never show gift ideas on the lock screen.
Gift price check (optional)Show about how much a gift idea costsOnly when you tap Check price on an idea that holds a shop link: the app downloads that public page from your device (only that address, no cookies, nothing from your notes) and reads the price the page states. Prices you type stay on your device and in your iCloud.
Share ideas as a web page (optional)Send a gift list or wish list to someoneYou choose the ideas. They travel inside the link, after the "#", which no server ever receives. The page is on the developer's static site; it holds only titles, links, prices and short notes, no photos and no contact details.
Scan an event (optional)Turn a flyer or screenshot into a note and calendar eventThe photo's text is read on your device. The photos stay in your note. Nothing is uploaded.
Widgets, Dynamic Island and Lock Screen countdownShow your next reminders, birthdays and a countdownA small summary (reminder titles unless you hide them, first names and birthday dates) is kept on your device for the widgets. It is not sent anywhere.
Unspool Plus purchase (optional)Unlock extra features with a one-time in-app purchaseHandled entirely by Apple (App Store and StoreKit). The developer receives no payment details, no name and no purchase record. The app only checks on your device whether Plus is unlocked.
NotificationsReminders and recipe timersScheduled on your device.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect, sell, share or track your data. There is no analytics, advertising or tracking code.
  • We don't have a server that receives your data. (If you use Cloud AI, text goes straight from your device to the provider you chose, never through us.)
  • We don't use your notes to train any model.

Your control

  • Every permission above is optional and can be changed in iOS Settings → Unspool.
  • You can export everything (Settings → Your data) and delete notes at any time. Turning off iCloud sync keeps your notes on this device; deleting the app removes them from this device, and deleting them in the app removes them from your iCloud copy.
  • You can delete the iCloud copy in iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Unspool.

Children

Unspool is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.
