Unspool

Unspool Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Unspool is a note-capture app. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and it doesn't track you. The developer does not receive your notes, recordings or any other data from the app.

What the app handles, and where it goes

DataWhyWhere it goes
Notes, text, photos, files, voice recordingsThey are your notesStored on your device. If iCloud sync is on (the default), they are also stored in your private iCloud account, encrypted by Apple's CloudKit. Only you and your signed-in devices can read them.
Microphone and speechRecording and transcribing voice notesTranscription happens on your device.
AI sorting (titles, tags, cleanup, reminders)Organise your notesDone by Apple Intelligence on your device. On iOS and macOS 27 and later, a long note, a question about your notes, a message to Chat with Spooly, or the words you give Untangle to split into threads, when your device can't handle it, may instead run on Apple's Private Cloud Compute (it is automatic, and choosing On device in Settings → AI turns it off; if you choose Private Cloud there, every note and question uses it; that choice shows when Show all settings, at the top of Settings, is on). Apple designs Private Cloud Compute so that your data isn't stored or accessible to Apple. Cloud AI with your own key (below) is separate and off by default.
Cloud AI with your own key (optional, off by default)Sort notes, tidy recipes, write recipe steps and fix mistakes with a model you choose, for example on a phone without Apple IntelligenceOnly if you turn it on in Settings → Cloud AI and add your own API key for Claude (Anthropic), OpenAI or Gemini (Google). Then, for the things you chose, the text of the note or recipe being worked on (and your tag names with a few short examples of notes filed under them) is sent from your device straight to that company with your key, under their terms and privacy policy, and is billed to your account. The key is kept only in this device's Keychain: it is not synced, not backed up and not seen by the developer. With a key added, a long note that holds several separate thoughts (a ramble) is also sent to be split into separate notes (voice dump); whether it looks like a ramble is decided on your device first, and nothing is sent for ordinary short notes. Untangle (say or type everything that's tangled up, then sort it one thread at a time) follows the same setting as notes: where your key is allowed for notes, the words you gave it may be sent to be split into threads. Its dictation runs on your device only. Voice recordings, photos, your other notes and recipes in the Family book are never sent, not even through a feature such as Fix this recipe. Google's terms require you to be 18 or older, and in the EEA, Switzerland and the UK a paid key (billing turned on); on a free Gemini key, Google may use what is sent to improve its products and people at Google may read it. You can turn it off or remove the key at any time.
Chat with Spooly (optional)Type to Spooly instead of talking: save notes, reminders and gift ideas, or ask about your own notes and peopleThe chat's messages are kept only on this device, in a file that is not synced to iCloud and not included in backups, and are deleted after 7 days or when you tap Clear chat (Settings → Spooly → More). When no AI can answer, what you type is saved as an ordinary note (stored like any note you type, in your iCloud if sync is on), with Undo under it. Each message goes to the AI that answers it (if one can't answer, the message goes on to the next one allowed), and the chat names the one that answered at the top and under every reply: Apple Intelligence on your device; Apple's Private Cloud Compute (iOS and macOS 27, unless Settings → AI is On device); or, only if Settings → Cloud AI already allows your own key for notes, the company of that key (Anthropic, OpenAI or Google), with your key, under their terms, billed to your account. Earlier messages are sent with a new one only to the AI that answered them. To answer a question it may look up a few matching notes or the one person you asked about, and those details go to the AI answering; your other notes, your list of people, your contacts and Family book recipes are never sent. On a free Gemini key (Google may use what is sent to improve its products, and people at Google may read it) it only saves things and looks nothing up, unless you say in Settings → Cloud AI, next to the Gemini key, that your Google project has billing on. The live voice setting and its consent are never used for the chat. Nothing goes to the developer.
Read a conversation (optional, Unspool Plus, needs your own key)Suggest details for a person's page from your texts with themOnly when you start it on a person's page and confirm the screen that follows. You pick an export of a conversation (a CSV, text or PDF file, screenshots, or a screen recording of the chat; the text of screenshots and recordings is read on your device). It is read in memory on your device and never stored; a recording picked from Photos is held in a temporary file only while it is read and deleted right after. Before anything is sent you see how many messages and which dates, whose messages are read as that person's (you can change it), and, in a group chat, that the other members' lines go too, as context. Phone numbers (including numbers written the way they are outside the US), email addresses and street addresses are blanked out on your device first (on by default); street addresses are recognised by a house number followed by a capitalised street name, so one written all in lower case may not be caught. Then that conversation, including the other person's messages and their name, and the details already listed under Good to know on their page (so they aren't suggested again; blanked out the same way when that is on), is sent from your device straight to the provider you chose in Cloud AI, with your own key, under their terms, and billed to your account. It is not available with a free Gemini key (Google may use and read what is sent), unless you say in Settings → Cloud AI, next to the Gemini key, that your Google project has billing on. Nothing goes to the developer. The results stay in your device's memory until you save or discard them (they are gone when Unspool is closed); if the read finishes while you are in another app, one quiet notification on your device says it is ready. Only the suggestions you accept are saved, to that person's page; the conversation itself is not kept. Unspool remembers only the date of the newest message read, so the next read can start after it.
Talk it through and spoken questions (optional)Answer a follow-up question by voice, talk a ramble through and have it turned into notes, reminders and gift ideas, or ask Spooly to do things in the app (it asks first and acts only on your yes)By default (Settings → Spooly → Spooly's voice → This phone) the conversation stays on your device: Apple's speech recognition and voices, and Apple Intelligence. Notes, reminders and gift ideas it saves are handled like any other note (see AI sorting above). Spoken follow-up questions are answered on your device only, and the app listens for an answer only when the question was read aloud; they are read aloud by Apple's voice unless you use live voice with "Use the OpenAI voice everywhere" (see below). Questions that name people from your People list ("Which Sam…?") are always read by Apple's voice.
Live voice with your own OpenAI key (optional, off by default)A faster, more natural voice conversation in Talk it throughLive voice uses OpenAI only. Only if you choose OpenAI in Settings → Spooly → Spooly's voice, add your own OpenAI key and confirm the screen that names the company. Then, only while Talk it through is open, what you say (as audio) is sent from your device straight to OpenAI with your key, and its spoken replies come back; the panel shows a line naming the company whenever this is on. If you ask about your notes or a person (for example "when's Sam's birthday"), the few details that answer that question are sent too; Family book recipes, your other notes and your contact list are never sent. If you ask Spooly to do something (open a screen, add a person you name, mark a reminder done or move it later, change a display setting, archive or delete a note, save a recipe you say out loud), the name you said, or the title of the note or reminder it found for that request, is sent the same way (for someone already in People, never their saved birthday or label), and nothing changes until you tap Yes on screen, or say yes as your first answer right after Spooly asks while Unspool is open on screen; entering keys, turning an AI on, sharing and purchases are never done by voice. It is billed to your account and covered by OpenAI's terms and privacy policy. The voice you hear is AI-generated. OpenAI keeps API data up to 30 days for abuse checks and does not train on it by default, and its terms ask that you are 18 or older or have a parent or guardian's permission. The terms are those of the person whose key it is; Unspool only uses the key you give it, for this feature, and keeps it on this device. Nothing goes to the developer. The key is kept only in this device's Keychain. Turning live voice off, or removing the key, stops it, and you are asked again if you turn it back on. Use the OpenAI voice everywhere (on while live voice is set up, and can be turned off in Settings → Spooly → More…): so Spooly sounds the same outside Talk it through, the words it says there (a question after a voice note, a cooking step of your own recipes read aloud in Cook mode; steps of Family book recipes are always read by Apple's voice on your device) are sent as text to OpenAI with your key, which sends back the sound in the voice you chose; nothing is stored, and if it fails Apple's voice reads them on your device. Those words can include bits of the note or recipe they are about, but never names from your People list: those questions are read by Apple's voice. Turning the setting off, turning live voice off or removing the key stops it at once. Answers spoken by Siri (cooking with Siri, Shortcuts) are always in Siri's own voice and are not sent. Gemini (Google) is not used for voice; a Gemini key is only used for Cloud AI and Chat with Spooly (see above).
Calendar (optional)Add plans from your notes to your calendar and keep them in stepStays on your devices (and your own iCloud Calendar, if you use it). Only a title, place and link to the note are written. The app only looks for events it created.
Contacts (optional)Link people you mention in a note to your contacts, and fill in a person's birthday and photoYou can add a person from a contact with Apple's contact picker. If you also allow Contacts access (optional, asked from a note or from Settings → Places & Calendar), the app matches names mentioned in your notes against your address book on your device to link the people you mention; to do that it can search the whole address book, but only on your device. For a contact you add a person from, its name, birthday and a small copy of the photo are copied onto the person's page and stored on your device and in your private iCloud (like your notes); its phone numbers and emails are not copied (pages added with earlier versions may still hold one). Nothing from your contacts goes to the developer or to any AI model. On the Mac there is no picker, so linking a contact there needs that access. You can also add people without any contact.
Location (optional)Find places for your notes; arrive/leave and leave-now remindersUsed on your device. To look up a place, the place name you wrote is sent to Apple Maps with the area around your home or last known location. "Leave now" alerts ask Apple Maps for the travel time between where you are and the place. "Always" access is used only for the arrive/leave reminders you turn on.
Link previewsShow a title and picture for a link in a noteApple's link-preview service fetches the page and its picture from your device when you open a note or the Board; the request goes to that site.
Recipes (#recipe notes)Turn a recipe you saved into ingredients and steps with timersCleaned up on your device by Apple Intelligence. If your note is a link, or contains one next to a #recipe, the app downloads that public web page from your device (only that address is requested, with no cookies and nothing from your notes) and reads the recipe on your device. A note that is only a link is also checked this way, in the background, to see whether it is a recipe; one-time links (sign-in, confirm, unsubscribe) are skipped. Photos of a recipe are read on your device. Timers are set with the system's alarm and notification features on your device. Hands-free Cook mode (off until you turn it on) listens for short spoken commands such as "next" using on-device speech recognition; nothing is recorded or kept. Nutrition estimates are calculated on your device from a nutrition table (USDA FoodData Central, public domain) stored inside the app; your ingredients are never sent anywhere. The shopping list is an ordinary checklist note in your own notes and iCloud.
Camera (optional)Scan a printed recipe pageUsed only when you tap "Scan a page". The photo is read on your device and stays in your note; it is not sent anywhere else.
Siri and ShortcutsVoice commands such as "Next step in Unspool" and starting a noteHandled on your device. The commands read the recipe you have open and can start its timer.
Family recipe book and shared shopping list (optional)Share recipes, and a shopping list, with people you inviteUses Apple's iCloud sharing. Only the recipes and the shopping list you put in the Family book are shared, only with the people you invite, and they can add and edit them. They are stored in your iCloud account (and in the invited people's iCloud for their copies); nothing goes to the developer. Leaving or stopping the share removes the copies.
People pages (optional)Keep gift ideas, things to ask, facts and birthdays for people you chooseOnly people you add get a page; nothing is imported in bulk. Matching a name in a note to a person happens on your device, and the list of people is never sent to an AI model, including the one you may add with your own key. The only exceptions are Read a conversation (see above), which you start yourself for one person, live voice (see above), which sends that one person's page only when you ask about them out loud, and Chat with Spooly (see above), which sends that one person's page to the AI answering only when you ask about them. People pages are stored on your device and in your private iCloud, encrypted by Apple's CloudKit. Birthday reminders are scheduled on your device and never show gift ideas or an age on the lock screen. Removing a person removes their page's details (photo, birthday, facts and any phone number or email) at once; only the name and label stay, as a removal marker, until it is cleared from iCloud after 30 days.
Gift price check (optional)Show about how much a gift idea costsOnly when you tap Check price on an idea that holds a shop link: the app downloads that public page from your device (only that address, no cookies, nothing from your notes) and reads the price the page states. Prices you type stay on your device and in your iCloud.

The page is on the developer's static site; it holds only titles, links and prices, plus an idea's own note only when you see it and switch it on for that idea; no photos and no contact details.
Scan an event (optional)Turn a flyer or screenshot into a note and calendar eventThe photo's text is read on your device. The photos stay in your note. Nothing is uploaded.
Widgets, Dynamic Island and Lock Screen countdownShow your next reminders, birthdays and a countdownA small summary (reminder titles unless you hide them, first names (with a surname's first letter when two people share one, never a label) and birthday dates) is kept on your device for the widgets. It is not sent anywhere.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect, sell, share or track your data. There is no analytics, advertising or tracking code.
  • We don't have a server that receives your data. (If you use Cloud AI, live voice or Chat with Spooly with your own key, text or audio goes straight from your device to the provider you chose, never through us.)
  • We don't use your notes to train any model.

Your control

  • Every permission above is optional and can be changed in iOS Settings → Unspool.
  • Some Settings groups named on this page (AI, Cloud AI, Places & Calendar) show only when Show all settings, at the top of Unspool's Settings, is on.
  • You can export everything (Settings → Your data) and delete notes at any time. Turning off iCloud sync keeps your notes on this device; deleting the app removes them from this device, and deleting them in the app removes them from your iCloud copy.
  • You can delete the iCloud copy in iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Unspool.

Children

Unspool is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.
